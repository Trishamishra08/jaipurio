Dubai FAQs

Questions Dubai buyers ask

Do you ship to Dubai?

Yes. Orders are packed in Jaipur and shipped by tracked courier to UAE & GCC, typically arriving in 5–10 working days.

Will I be charged customs duty or import tax on arrival?

Most personal shipments clear without additional duty under GCC personal-import allowances, but this varies by item value — confirm for high-value marble or jewellery orders.

How long does delivery take?

Typically 5–10 working days from dispatch, depending on the item and customs clearance time.

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery. Buyer covers return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or not as described.

Can I customize a marble idol's size or finish?

Select idols and jewellery pieces can be customized on size, finish or engraving. Message us before ordering to confirm feasibility and any extra shipping cost.
