Privacy Policy

How Jaipurio collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you shop or sell on our Rajasthani handicraft marketplace.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaipurio ("Jaipurio", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you visit our website, use our app, buy from our marketplace or sell on it. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. By using Jaipurio you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address, gender and, if you choose, date of birth.
  • Delivery details: shipping addresses, pincode, city, state and the contact number for delivery.
  • Orders and payments: the products you buy, order value, payment method and payment status. Card, UPI and net-banking details are entered on our payment partner's secure page. Jaipurio does not see or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Communication: messages you send through our contact form, WhatsApp, email or phone, and product reviews and ratings you post.
  • Sellers (vendors): owner name, business name, GST number, business address, bank account and IFSC details, UPI ID and any KYC documents uploaded for verification.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, products viewed and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cart, wishlist and login information saved on your device (cookies and local storage) so the site remembers you. See our Cookie Policy.
  • A notification token, if you allow order notifications on your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including sending a one-time password (OTP) to your mobile number to log you in.
  • To process, pack, ship and deliver your orders, collect cash on delivery, and handle returns and refunds.
  • To send order updates (order placed, shipped, delivered) by SMS, email, WhatsApp or app notification.
  • To answer your questions and resolve complaints.
  • To verify sellers, list their products, and pay them their earnings.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access, and to keep the platform safe.
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To send offers and newsletters, only if you have agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax (GST) and accounting requirements.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed, with:

  • Sellers on Jaipurio: your name, delivery address and phone number, so the seller can pack and ship your order.
  • Courier and logistics partners: delivery name, address and phone number.
  • Payment partners (such as Razorpay and Cashfree): to process online payments and refunds.
  • Service providers: SMS/OTP, email, notification, cloud hosting and file storage companies that work for us under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government and law enforcement: when required by law, court order or to protect the rights and safety of Jaipurio, our customers or others.

Public reviews show your name and rating along with your review.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. Order, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (usually 8 years). When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

5. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, secure servers and trusted payment gateways. Only authorised staff can see personal data, and only when needed for their work. No system is fully secure, so please keep your OTP private and never share it with anyone, including people claiming to be from Jaipurio.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • See and correct your account details from My Account, or ask us to do it.
  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal data, except records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing messages at any time.
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (below), and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

Jaipurio is meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Links to other websites

Our site may link to other websites (for example social media or partner brands). Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown on the website.
